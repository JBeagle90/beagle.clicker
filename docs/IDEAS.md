# Ideas

Small, fun ideas for when no suggestion has bones. Each one fits in one update. Remove what gets built,
add ones that fit the game as it is now, and keep at most 15.

- Golden bone: now and then a golden bone pops up near the beagle; tapping it gives a burst of bones.
- A fourth upgrade, Bone Bakery: 75 bones a second, first one costs 8,000.
- Milestones: a cheerful message at 100, 1,000 and 10,000 bones earned.
- The beagle's tail wags faster the more pups and parks you own.
- A gentle "boop" sound on each pat, with a sound on/off button that's remembered.
- Stats in the save panel: total pats, total bones earned, time played.
- The beagle blinks now and then, and looks happier as bones grow.
- A daily treat: once a day, a free bonus of a minute's worth of bones.
